## To Players: Information for CTFd

### Description
**Challenge Overview**

You seem to have found myself at the bottom of an old well, with no obvious way to get out. The only thing you can find on the ground is a pile of bones next to a dusty old personal assistant device. You don't see its owner anywhere, so surely they managed to escape.

You try scraping the device for clues, but almost everything is encrypted. The only usable artefact left behind came from the model's internals, a cache of some sort. You hope this is enough to get somewhere.
